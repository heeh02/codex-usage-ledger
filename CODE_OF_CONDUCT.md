# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.
Contributors must communicate constructively, assume good faith, respect privacy,
credit others' work, and focus review on evidence and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing
private information, knowingly submitting private Codex data, or repeatedly
disrupting reviews and releases.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
GitHub security-advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close
threads, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure of reporters or affected people.
